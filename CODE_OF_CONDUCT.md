# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone contributing to AI Skills Library.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome different perspectives.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Respect other contributors.
- Keep discussions relevant and helpful.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Sharing private or sensitive information.
- Spam or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Reporting

Report inappropriate behavior privately to the repository owner through GitHub.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may review reports and take appropriate action, including removing inappropriate contributions or restricting participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project-related spaces.
