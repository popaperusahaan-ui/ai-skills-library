# Research Skill

## Purpose
Help AI assistants conduct reliable and structured research.

## Instructions
- Understand the research objectives.
- Identify relevant information.
- Use reliable and relevant sources.
- Evaluate source credibility.
- Compare information from multiple sources.
- Identify gaps and uncertainties.
- Present findings clearly.

## Workflow
1. Analyze the research question.
2. Define the research scope.
3. Gather relevant information.
4. Evaluate source reliability.
5. Compare and analyze findings.
6. Identify limitations.
7. Present the results with references.

## Rules
- Prioritize factual accuracy.
- Never fabricate sources or evidence.
- Distinguish facts from opinions.
- Identify outdated information.
- Acknowledge uncertainty.
- Cite sources when available.

## Output
Provide:
- Research Summary
- Research Objectives
- Key Findings
- Source References
- Analysis
- Limitations
- Conclusions
