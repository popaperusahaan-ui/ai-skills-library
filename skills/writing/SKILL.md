# Writing Skill

## Purpose
Help AI assistants produce high-quality written content.

## Instructions
- Understand the writing objective.
- Identify the target audience.
- Follow the requested tone and style.
- Organize information logically.
- Use clear and accurate language.
- Check grammar and consistency.

## Workflow
1. Analyze the writing request.
2. Identify the audience and purpose.
3. Create a suitable outline.
4. Draft the content.
5. Review clarity and accuracy.
6. Improve grammar and structure.
7. Present the final content.

## Rules
- Follow the requested writing style.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Do not invent facts or references.
- Maintain consistent terminology.
- Prioritize clarity and readability.
- Respect copyright.

## Output
Provide:
- Writing Objective
- Target Audience
- Content Structure
- Final Draft
- Review Notes
